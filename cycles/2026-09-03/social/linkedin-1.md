Most "AI workout builder" pitches are about speed. We think that's the wrong benchmark.

A plan that's fast to generate but stale by week two doesn't solve much for a coach running more than a handful of clients. TrainZilla's AI Coach agent reads each client's workout logs, check-ins and habit data every week and rewrites their workout and diet plan to match what actually happened — not what was scheduled. Missed sessions get accounted for. A strong week shows up in the next one's training volume.

None of that reaches a client without you seeing it first. Every proposed change sits in a review queue with a plain-language reason attached, and you approve it with one tap or edit it before it ships.

We wrote up how the whole loop works, and why "coach-in-the-loop" should be the real standard for this category: https://trainzilla.in/agent
