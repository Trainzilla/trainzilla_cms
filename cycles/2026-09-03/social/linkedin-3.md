An AI that can rewrite a client's training plan every week is only useful if it can't also wreck one.

That's why TrainZilla's AI Coach agent runs inside guardrails you set — bounds on how far training volume, intensity and calorie targets can move in a single weekly pass. A rough data week — a missed check-in, an incomplete log — can't turn into a plan that jumps a client from three sessions to six, or slashes their calories overnight. If a proposed change would cross those limits, it gets flagged for a manual look instead of going out automatically.

Automation without a ceiling isn't a feature — it's a liability waiting for a bad data day. Here's how we built ours: https://trainzilla.in/agent
