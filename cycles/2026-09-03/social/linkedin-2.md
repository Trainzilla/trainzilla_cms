Here's a question worth asking before you trust any AI tool with client programming: what happens when it's wrong?

Most AI workout builders treat "a coach can review it" as an assumption, not a design decision. TrainZilla's AI fitness coach is built the other way around — every weekly plan update goes into a review queue first. You see the diff, a one-line reason for the change, and you approve or edit it. Nothing lands on a client's app until you've signed off, every single week, not just when the plan was first created.

That standing checkpoint is the difference between "AI that suggests" and AI you can actually hand real client relationships to.
