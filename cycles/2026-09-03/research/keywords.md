Method: web SERP inspection (WebSearch), no Ahrefs connector this cycle — see stub below.

## Primary keyword

**AI workout builder for coaches**

Chosen over a broader term like "AI workout plan generator" because the page-1
SERP for that broader term is a wall of roundup listicles (see `competitive.md`)
that a single new article won't out-rank quickly. "AI workout builder for
coaches" is the head term the strongest single competitor page
(`resources.trainerize.com/ai-workout-builder`) actually targets, and it's
realistic to compete on with a differentiated angle: none of the incumbents
market the approval/guardrail/explainability loop TrainZilla actually ships.

## SERP feature notes

- Page 1 for the broad head terms ("AI workout plan generator", "AI workout
  builder for coaches") is dominated by **roundup listicles** ("Best AI
  Workout Builder Software for Coaches", "Best AI Personal Trainer Apps
  2026") from Trainerize, Ray, Sensai, Dr. Muscle, thetooljury — few single-
  product deep dives.
- Broad queries also trigger AI-generated answer summaries ahead of organic
  results, which favour clearly structured, directly-answering H2s (definition-
  style first sentence) over marketing copy — informs the article's structure.
- No featured snippet currently locked for "how does AI adjust a workout plan"
  or the guardrails/explainability long-tail terms — winnable with a clean,
  direct-answer H2.
- No video pack observed for these queries.
