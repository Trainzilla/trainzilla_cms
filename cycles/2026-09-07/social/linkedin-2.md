Opening a second location changes what you need from your software — not just how much of it you need.

A single-site gym mostly needs to keep its own clients on track. Add a second or tenth location and the job becomes comparing sites, standardising how coaches onboard clients, and rolling the same plan or habit template out everywhere instead of rebuilding it each time.

TrainZilla is built for solo trainers, online coaches, and multi-site gyms on one platform — AI plan-building, client management, sessions, payments, payouts, and a revenue dashboard that scales with you as you add locations.
