Switching gym software with clients spread across several locations is the part most owners put off — understandably.

That's why bulk CSV client import exists: bring your whole roster in with one upload instead of re-entering every client, location by location, by hand. Reassign coaches across sites and keep every client's plans, logs and check-ins in one place once you're in.

It's a small feature next to a revenue dashboard or automated payouts, but it's usually the one that decides whether a multi-location team actually makes the switch.

See how client management works across locations: https://trainzilla.in/help/client-management
