# Keyword & SERP research — Gym / franchise operations (focus week 37, 37 % 6 = 1)

Primary keyword and supporting set target this cycle's article + the 5 seoPages
refreshes. Volume/difficulty are **estimated from SERP inspection** (Ahrefs is
not connected — see the stub at the bottom).

## Primary keyword

**`multi-location gym management software`**

- Intent: transactional/comparison ("software" head term, buyer actively evaluating vendors) → maps to `funnelStage: decision`.
- Difficulty: **high** — page 1 is dominated by established, well-funded SaaS (ABC Ignite/Glofox, Mindbody, FLiiP, Helios, Zenoti roundups) with dedicated "multi-location" landing pages and comparison content.
- Volume band: **moderate** — a recognised buying-committee term for gym owners/operators running 2+ locations, not a mass-market query, but consistently searched by an in-market audience.

## SERP feature notes

- No obvious featured snippet on the primary keyword — results are a mix of
  vendor landing pages and "top N" roundup posts (GetApp, Zenoti, ByzFunder,
  1club.ai). A well-structured H2-per-question article has a real shot at a
  snippet on the narrower "how do you track revenue per gym location" query.
- No video pack on the head term.
- Page 1 is **listicle/roundup-heavy** for "best gym management software…"
  variants, but the primary keyword itself pulls more single-vendor landing
  pages (Glofox, FLiiP, Helios, Exercise.com) than roundups — an opening for a
  genuinely educational (not just a vendor pitch) piece.
