# Webinar topic brief — proposal only (not a CMS write)

**Working title:** "From Solo to Small Team: Habit & Plan Templates That Scale
With You"

**Abstract (2 sentences):** A working session for solo personal trainers and
small studio owners on building a habit and plan template library once, then
watching it apply automatically as the client roster grows or a second coach
joins. Live walkthrough of master habits, plan templates, and bulk CSV import
used together during an actual coach-onboarding scenario.

**5-point outline:**

1. Why "set it up once per client" breaks down somewhere between 10 and 30
   clients — and breaks down immediately the moment a second coach joins.
2. Building a master habit library: cadence, targets, and what changes when
   you edit a habit that's already assigned to clients.
3. Plan templates as the equivalent mechanism for workouts and diets — reuse,
   don't rebuild.
4. Bringing in a second coach: bulk CSV import of an existing roster, plus
   what that coach inherits on day one without being re-taught the system.
5. Live Q&A: what doesn't scale yet, and what coaches wish worked differently
   as they grow.

**Primary keyword it would target:** `personal training software for growing
teams` (see `research/keywords.md`).

**Feature anchor:** Master habits (define once, assign to many) `[GAP]`,
paired with Plan templates `[PARTIAL]` and Bulk CSV client import `[PARTIAL]`.

**Relationship to existing webinars:** Complements rather than replaces any of
the 6 webinars in the current snapshot (`inputs/webinars.json`) — none of the
existing titles cover habit/plan template reuse or multi-coach onboarding
specifically; the closest overlap is general AI-coach programming content,
which this would sit alongside rather than duplicate.
