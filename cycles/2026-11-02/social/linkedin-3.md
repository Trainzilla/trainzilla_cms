Most "coaching software features" pages read like a checklist: plans, check-ins, payments, an AI agent. What they usually skip is the mechanism that decides whether those features still work once you're not a one-person operation anymore.

Master habits are that mechanism for the habit side of coaching: define a habit once, roll it out to your whole roster, and keep editing it in one place as clients — or coaches — get added. It's a small detail that only matters once you're scaling, which is exactly when it matters most.
