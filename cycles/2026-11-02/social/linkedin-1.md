Every coaching app lets you assign a habit to a client. Fewer let you edit that habit once and have the change reach every client it's already assigned to.

That's the real difference between bulk assignment — a one-time push to a group — and a master habit definition: a live reference every assigned client keeps pointing back to. If you've ever recreated the same bedtime or hydration habit for the fifth, tenth or thirtieth client, or watched a new coach rebuild your habit library from memory, you've felt that gap.

We wrote up how TrainZilla's master habits compare to the usual bulk-assign approach, and what it means once a second coach joins your roster.
