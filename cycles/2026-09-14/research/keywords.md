## SERP feature notes

- No obvious featured snippet currently owned for the primary long-tail phrase —
  open for a well-structured H2/FAQ answer.
- First page for the broader "meal swap app" queries is dominated by consumer
  App Store / Play Store listings (Prospre, MacroPlan, Hit My Macros) and
  general "best macro tracking apps" listicles — not coach-software content.
  That's the gap: nobody is writing this for the *coach*, only for the
  self-tracking dieter.
- For "flexible meal planner coaching software", Trainerize's own blog post and
  product page rank — a single-vendor angle, not a neutral how-to.
