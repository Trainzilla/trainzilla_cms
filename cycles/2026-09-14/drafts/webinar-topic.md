# Webinar topic proposal — cycle 2026-09-14

**Working title:** Building Meal Plans That Survive Real Life: Swaps, Macro
Deltas, and How Much Flexibility to Give Each Client

**Abstract (2 sentences):** Most nutrition plans don't fail on day one — they
fail the first time a client swaps a meal and the coach never finds out. This
session walks through building meal plans at the ingredient level, setting
pre-approved swap options, and using a macro-delta preview so flexibility
never turns into a blind spot.

**5-point outline:**

1. Why rigid meal plans lose adherence first, not last.
2. What a like-for-like swap is, and how to build a swap list per meal instead
   of a single fixed option.
3. Live walkthrough: building a meal from real ingredients (protein, carb,
   fat, oil) instead of a single calorie/macro total.
4. Setting swap boundaries by client stage — tighter early in a phase, looser
   once a client has shown consistency.
5. Reading the macro-delta log across a full client roster without opening
   every food diary.

**Primary keyword targeted:** "how to let clients swap meals without breaking
their macros" (same as this cycle's new article — the webinar can promote and
extend it).

**Feature anchor:** Meal swap with macro-delta preview `[GAP]`; supported by
Per-ingredient macro breakdown `[PARTIAL]` (docs/feature-inventory.md).

**Relationship to existing webinars:** Complements *"Vegetarian Sports
Nutrition for Indian Athletes"* (from the snapshot) by generalizing the
swap/macro-delta mechanic across all diet types rather than one cuisine —
