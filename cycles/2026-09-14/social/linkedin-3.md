Vegetarian meal plans get harder to keep flexible, not easier — swap chicken for tofu and the macros barely move; swap dal for paneer and they move a lot more than most plans account for.

Our guide to vegetarian nutrition planning now covers building swap-ready plant-based meals with the macro delta shown before a client commits to a trade — so "client swapped paneer for dal" doesn't quietly turn into a macro gap nobody notices until the next check-in.

The same logic that works for chicken-and-rice plans applies here: build the meal from real ingredients, give two or three approved swaps, and let the numbers do the checking instead of a coach's memory.

If you coach a mostly vegetarian client base, this is worth a read:
