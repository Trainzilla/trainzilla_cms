We're scoping a webinar on a problem every coach with a nutrition offering has run into: a meal plan that only works if the client never deviates from it.

The session will walk through building meals from real ingredients, setting pre-approved swap options instead of a single fixed meal, and reading a macro-delta log across a full client roster — without reopening every food diary.

It's built to complement our existing session on vegetarian sports nutrition, not replace it — this one covers the swap mechanic across any diet style, for any coach whose clients don't always eat exactly what's on the plan.
