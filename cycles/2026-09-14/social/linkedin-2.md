Ask most coaches how they handle a client's food swap mid-week, and the honest answer is: badly. A message, a mental estimate, hope the macros land close enough.

TrainZilla's diet plan builder stores every meal at the ingredient level — the rice, the dal, the cooking oil, each its own line — so when a client swaps one for another, the exact calorie and macro delta shows up before the swap is confirmed. No guessing, no waiting for the next check-in to find out the day drifted.

It's a small mechanical change with a real effect on adherence: flexibility that doesn't cost the coach visibility. Once a meal is built from real ingredients — the protein, the carb, even the cooking oil as its own line — a swap stops being a guess and becomes an exact calculation.
