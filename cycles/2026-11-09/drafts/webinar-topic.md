# Webinar topic proposal — cycle 2026-11-09

**Working title:** From Overtraining Signs to a Deload Call: Reading Recovery
Data Without Overreacting

**Abstract:** Coaches have always looked for overtraining by feel — a stalled
lift, a client who's harder to motivate. This session shows how to read the
same signal cluster in sleep and heart-rate data days earlier, how much to
actually pull back for a deload, and how to let an AI Coach make that call
automatically without losing the final say.

**Outline:**
1. The signs of overtraining that show up together, and why one alone never means much.
2. Functional overreaching vs. overtraining syndrome: which one you're actually looking at, and why the distinction changes what you do next.
3. How much to pull back for a deload, and how long a proper one usually takes to resolve.
4. Setting guardrails: bounding how far an automatic plan change is allowed to go, so a bad week nudges the plan instead of rewriting it.
5. Live walkthrough: TrainZilla's AI Coach agent dialling back a sample plan from a synced Apple Health / Health Connect recovery dip, with the plain-language rationale and the coach's one-tap approval step.

**Primary keyword targeted:** signs a client needs a deload week (supporting: when to deload, overtraining syndrome)

**Feature anchor:** Recovery signals feeding the AI Coach `[GAP]`, with Guardrails on AI plan changes `[GAP]` covered in point 4 and "Why this changed" rationale `[GAP]` covered in point 5.

**Relationship to existing webinars:** Complements `ai-fitness-coaching`
("AI-Powered Fitness Coaching for Indian Trainers") rather than replacing it —
that session covers AI coaching broadly; this one goes deep on the specific
