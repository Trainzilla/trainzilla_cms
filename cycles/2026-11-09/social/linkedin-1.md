Most coaches catch overtraining the same way: a lift that used to move well suddenly feels heavy, or a client who never misses a session skips two in a row. By then the fatigue has usually been building for a week.

The signs actually show up earlier than that — in sleep and resting heart-rate trends, days before performance drops. The question is whether anything's watching for them.

We just published a guide on reading that cluster of signs, how much to actually pull back for a deload, and how TrainZilla's AI Coach can make that call itself — within bounded guardrails, with a plain-language reason, and your one-tap approval before a client ever sees the change.
