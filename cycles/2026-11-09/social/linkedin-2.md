Your clients are already generating recovery data. The question is whether it ever reaches you.

TrainZilla's free client app syncs with Apple Health on iOS and Health Connect on Android, so sleep duration and resting heart rate flow in automatically alongside their normal workout and diet logging — nothing extra for the client to track, no second app to open.

That sync isn't just a dashboard number. It's the data pipe that feeds the AI Coach's weekly plan rewrite, so a bad sleep week can show up as a smaller, bounded pull-back on volume or intensity — before a stalled lift makes it obvious.
