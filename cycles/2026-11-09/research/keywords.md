## People also ask / related questions

No Ahrefs or SERP-scrape tool is connected this cycle, so these are drawn from
the headlines and body content of the top-ranking pages found via web search
(NASM, My PT Hub, Jefit, OPEX, Cyclingnews, Tonal, Active.com), not a literal
PAA-box scrape:

1. What are the signs of overtraining?
2. How do I know when to take a deload week?
3. What's the difference between functional overreaching and overtraining syndrome?
4. How much should training volume or intensity drop during a deload?
5. How long does it take to recover from overtraining?
6. Should training stop completely during a deload, or just get lighter?
7. How often should a deload week happen in a training block?
8. Can a deload week cause a client to lose progress?

These become H2/H3 candidates in the article.

## SERP feature notes

- No single domain visibly owns a featured snippet for "signs of overtraining"
  or "when to deload" — page 1 is a mix of listicles ("Top 5 signs…"),
  certification-body explainers (NASM), and SaaS vendor content blogs.
- No video pack observed in the result set returned.
- Page 1 for the deload cluster is **not** dominated by coach-software
  comparison content — it's consumer/athlete-framed fitness media and vendor
  blogs. Nobody is writing this for a coach managing a roster of clients
  through a platform. That's the opening (see `competitive.md`).
