The warning signs of overtraining show up in the gym LAST. 📉

By the time a lift stalls or a client starts skipping sessions, the underlying fatigue has usually been building for a while. Sleep and heart-rate trends move first — often days earlier.

That's why your dashboard should show more than workout and diet logs: sleep, HRV and resting heart rate, right next to the sessions, so the pattern shows up before the plateau does.

One view. Every client. Nothing to chase down at the next check-in.
