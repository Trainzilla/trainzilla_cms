## SERP feature notes

- "Best AI coaching software" roundups are dominated by vendor and affiliate
  blogs (Trainerize, Trainerfu, Everfit via secondary coverage, fitnessmentors,
  unite.ai) — no obvious featured snippet owner, no video pack observed in the
  fetched results.
- "Can AI replace a personal trainer" and "is an AI workout plan safe" pull
  consumer/trade press (NBC, Tom's Guide, Women's Fitness, ACE Fitness,
  rollingout) rather than SaaS vendors — a different, more skeptical audience
  than the comparison-shopper hitting the primary keyword.
- No competitor page we found targets "coach-in-the-loop" *and* a per-change
  rationale *and* a numeric guardrail together — see `competitive.md` for the
  content-gap breakdown.
