A question worth asking before you connect any AI agent to your coaching data: when it changes something, does it tell you why?

Most "AI adjusts your plan" features ship with a confidence score at best, or nothing at all. The client — and often the coach — just notices the plan looks different one week and not the next.

TrainZilla's AI agent gets secure, scoped access to a client's plans, habits, and check-ins, but every write it proposes comes with a plain-language reason attached, and every change is bounded so it can nudge a plan, not rewrite it. You review before anything ships, and you actually know what you're approving — not just that a change happened somewhere in the background.

More on how the integration works: https://trainzilla.in/solutions/ai-agent
