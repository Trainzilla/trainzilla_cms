Every "best AI coaching software" roundup this year says roughly the same thing: the AI builds the plan, the AI adjusts the plan. Almost none of them say whether a human sees the change before the client does, or why it was made.

We looked at what the bigger platforms actually publish. A few (Trainerize among them) now call this "coach-in-the-loop" — AI drafts, coach approves. Good baseline. But approving fifteen clients' changes every Monday with no explanation attached is still a chore dressed up as oversight.

TrainZilla's AI Coach adds the two pieces we couldn't find documented anywhere else together: a plain-language reason on every single edit, and a numeric guardrail on how far volume, intensity, or calories can move in one pass. One tap approves it — or doesn't.
