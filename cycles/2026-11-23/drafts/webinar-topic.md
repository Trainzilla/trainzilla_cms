# Webinar topic proposal — 2026-11-23

**Working title:** "Inside the AI Coach: What It Changes, Why, and Where It Stops"

**Abstract:** A walkthrough of how TrainZilla's AI Coach drafts weekly plan
adjustments from check-in and tracking data, and exactly what stands between
a drafted change and a client seeing it — the coach's one-tap approval queue,
the plain-language "why this changed" reason on every edit, and the numeric
guardrails that cap how far volume, intensity, or calories can move in one
pass.

**Outline:**

1. The problem with "AI adjusts your plan" as a feature line — what coaches
   are actually worried about (silent changes, no accountability, no cap).
2. Live walkthrough: a client's check-in and workout logs come in, the AI
   drafts a change, and the coach sees it land in their review queue with its
   rationale attached.
3. The guardrails in practice — what's bounded, and a couple of before/after
   examples using the AI Coach's own proposed-vs-applied values (no
   fabricated client data — use the product's own demo/sandbox account).
4. Q&A prompt: "What would you want the AI to never be allowed to do to a
   plan without asking you first?" — collect live for a future content cycle.
5. Close: where this fits for a solo coach vs. a multi-coach gym (the
   approval queue works the same per-coach either way).

**Primary keyword it targets:** best AI coaching software for personal
trainers (same primary as this cycle's article — the webinar is the
live-demo complement to a read-it-first comparison piece).

**Feature anchor:** Coach-in-the-loop review `[GAP]`, with "Why this
changed" rationale `[GAP]` and Guardrails on AI plan changes `[GAP]` as the
two things actually demoed live.

**Relationship to existing webinars:** `inputs/webinars.json` lists six
existing webinars, including "AI-Powered Fitness Coaching for Indian
Trainers." This proposal **complements** that one rather than replacing it:
the existing webinar is India-market-framed and broader (AI coaching as a
whole); this one is global-English, keyword-targeted at the comparison
shopper, and narrow — it demos exactly the review/explain/guardrail mechanics
this cycle's article and seoPages refreshes argue for, with nothing
India-specific in the framing per the Global English hard rule. The other
five (home-business, marketing, nutrition, monsoon, senior-fitness
specialization) are unrelated clusters. Final placement call is for the human
reviewer, since webinar topics aren't a CMS write this cycle.
