Most nutrition plans don't fail on day one. They fail the first time a client swaps a meal and never tells you.

We just published a practical guide on building meal plans that survive that moment: store every meal as real ingredients (protein, carb, fat, oil — not one calorie total), give each meal two or three pre-approved swap options instead of an open list, and show the macro delta the second a client picks a swap — not three days later at check-in.

That last part is the piece most coaching software skips. A macro-tracking app will recalculate a swap for the person using it. What a coach actually needs is to see what changed across a whole roster of clients, without opening every food diary.

Full breakdown, including a per-ingredient walkthrough, is on the blog.
