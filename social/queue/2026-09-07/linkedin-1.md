Most multi-location gym software can tell you which location is behind on revenue this month. Almost none of them can tell you which coach at that location is behind.

That's the gap we wrote about this week: per-location reporting is table stakes now. The useful layer is per-coach — seeing, in the same dashboard, whether a slow month is a location problem or one coach's roster shrinking. No more cross-referencing a location report against a separate staff spreadsheet to find out.

We broke down what a franchise or multi-location gym should actually expect from its software — revenue by coach, payouts that don't need an invoice, and attendance tied to the numbers a coach is credited with.
