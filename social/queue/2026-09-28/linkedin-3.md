A question we hear from coaches evaluating any kind of automated plan adjustment: "how far is it allowed to go on its own?"

Fair question. It's the one we're building a webinar around: reading sleep, HRV and training-load data without overreacting to a single bad night — how many data points before you act, how much to pull back volume or intensity for a given signal, and how to explain the change to a client in one honest sentence instead of a black-box "the algorithm decided."

If recovery data is something you're already looking at (or thinking about looking at) for your clients, this is for you.
