Most "recovery tracking" in coaching software stops at a dashboard. Sleep and HRV numbers show up on screen, and the coach still has to notice them, interpret them, and manually pull the program back.

We just published a guide on what should happen next: when a client's sleep and heart-rate data point to under-recovery, the plan itself should adjust — within guardrails, not with a wild swing, and with a coach approving the change before it ever reaches the client.

That's the difference between recovery *tracking* and recovery-*aware* programming: one shows you the numbers, the other changes what happens next week because of them.
