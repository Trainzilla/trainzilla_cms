Set it up once. Not once per client. 💪

New on the blog: habit tracking software for personal trainers — and why "bulk assign" isn't the same as a habit that actually scales.

Most apps let you push a habit to a group of clients in one tap. Almost none let you edit that habit afterward and have it update everywhere it's already assigned.

Define the cadence and target once.
Roll it out to your whole roster.
Change it in one place when it needs to change — for everyone, not one client at a time.

That's the difference between busywork and a system that grows with you.
